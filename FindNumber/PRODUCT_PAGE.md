# FindNumber App Store Product Page

## Header and Search Results

**App name**  
Find Number

**Subtitle**  
Spot numbers. Beat your time.

**Search result treatment**

- Lead with the app icon and the name **Find Number**.
- Show the subtitle **Spot numbers. Beat your time.** directly below the name.
- Use a clean, high-contrast icon that suggests a numbered grid; avoid small details that disappear at search-result size.
- Make the first screenshot legible at thumbnail size and show the game in progress, with the next number to find clearly visible.

**Search keywords**  
number, puzzle, focus, grid, reflex, speed

## App Previews and Screenshots

### App Preview storyboard

Create a short, silent gameplay preview using actual captured gameplay:

1. Open on the board-size picker, then select a grid.
2. Show the prompt (“Find 1”) and timer as the shuffled number grid appears.
3. Tap numbers in ascending order; show completed tiles changing color.
4. Finish the board and reveal the completion time and best-time result.
5. End on the board-size picker with the message: **Find your next best time.**

Keep transitions quick and the numbers large enough to read on a phone. Do not imply multiplayer, hints, or features that are not in the game.

### Screenshot sequence

Use portrait captures from the current app build. Keep any optional headline overlays outside the captured interface and keep the game UI unobstructed. The supplied screenshot files are clean captures without added headline text.

| Order | Screen to capture | Suggested headline |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Board-size picker showing 5×5, 7×7, and 10×10 | **Choose your challenge** |
| 2 | Active 5×5 game with “Find 1” and the timer visible | **Find the numbers in order** |
| 3 | Active board with several completed tiles highlighted | **Keep your eyes on the next number** |
| 4 | Active 10×10 game showing the larger grid | **Ready for a bigger challenge?** |
| 5 | Finished game showing the completion time and “New Record!” when available | **Beat your best time** |
| 6 | Best Times history screen with saved times for each grid size | **Your best times, at a glance** |

For the completion screenshot, use a fresh run that sets a record so the “New Record!” state is visible. The app may show a banner ad after a completed game; capture a clean completion screen before the ad appears, or use another screen for that shot.
